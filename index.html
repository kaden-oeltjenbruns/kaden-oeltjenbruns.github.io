About

For the past three years, I've built my career resolving operational crises — the kind where slow analysis costs a shut-down line, a lost contract, or a broken promise to a customer. That work has, so far, lived inside automotive manufacturing and supply chains. The problem-solving it trained into me doesn't stay there.

My work sits at the intersection of hands-on execution and executive accountability: running root-cause and capacity studies one day, presenting recovery status to senior leadership the next. I've built that range working alongside teams from Mexico, Germany, Switzerland, and the US, operating in Spanish, German, and English.

B.S. in Economics, minor in Mathematics, University of Minnesota — an analytical foundation I've since applied inside distribution centers, plant floors, and supplier war rooms rather than spreadsheets alone.
